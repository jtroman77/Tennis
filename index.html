How the lean works

Under 2.5 means two completed sets; Over 2.5 means three. Only explicitly identified BO3 singles with normal deciding sets are supported. BO5, unknown formats, shortened sets and match-tiebreak formats are excluded.

The score combines each player's observed two/three-set history from the last year. Same-surface results receive full weight, other surfaces 35%; older results decay with a 120-day half-life. Six neutral prior observations reduce small-sample extremes. Shared matches count once in the combined estimate. The model checks recent-only and surface-only estimates and both player profiles before qualifying a pick.

A qualifier needs at least 12 valid matches and 6 on this surface for EACH player, 20 unique matches overall, an effective weighted sample of 16, recent data, agreement between profiles and a conservative lower bound above 55. Thresholds are engineering rules, not validated accuracy guarantees. Lean is not a calibrated win probability. The original Total-Sets arithmetic is unchanged. Pointer Lean is separate and only runs after the Total-Sets qualifier gate, reducing API usage. It uses recent same-surface point tape for service points won, return points won and valuable/pressure points. If tape is insufficient, no player direction is guessed. Injury and travel still require separate review. No bet is placed by this page.
